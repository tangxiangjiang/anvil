## Goals

p_0.46.21 made the working mark 18px and orange. Measured against what is actually drawn,
that was half a fix: the shapes occupied about three quarters of the view box across and
below half to three quarters of it vertically, because the anvil sits low and the hammer's
head sits high in a 16-unit box whose corners were never used. The spark's strokes were
0.8 units — 0.9px at 18px — which the browser antialiases into a grey smudge, in the one
moment the mark exists to show.

The fix is in the drawing, not the box.

1. **Draw to the box.** The anvil and hammer are re-laid in the same 16-unit view box so
   their ink spans nearly all of it: the anvil grows a face, a waist and a base across the
   bottom, the hammer's handle is wider and its head larger and raised, and the horn reaches
   the left edge. The mark's box stays 18px; what changes is how much of it is ink.
2. **Make every stroke a pixel.** The spark's strokes go from 0.8 to 1.4 units (about 1.6px
   at 18px), and its rays lengthen so the strike reads before it fades.
3. **Keep the swing inside the box.** The head still pivots at the anvil's right edge; at
   `-72deg` it lands over the face, and every corner of it stays inside the view box.
4. **Keep the pivot honest.** The pivot moves with the drawing — `11.4px 9.8px`, the top
   right corner of the anvil's face — so the hammer turns where it rests, not where the old
   shapes did.

## Scope

`editors/vscode/media/chat.js` (`hammerGlyph`'s shapes and stroke), `media/chat.css` (the
pivot), `test/unit/working.test.ts`. The milestone page and the design chapter record the
change. No protocol field, no host change, no setting, no size change.

## Decisions (before any change)

- **Redraw rather than enlarge again.** A larger box with the same shapes would scale the
  empty margins too, and the mark is already larger than its text. The measured problem is
  the drawing's use of its box, so the drawing changes and the box does not.
- **Strokes measured in screen pixels, not units.** SVG strokes scale with the view box; a
  1px line has to be specified in units and checked at the size the stylesheet declares. The
  spark is the smallest thing in the mark and the first to vanish, so it sets the floor.
- **The pivot follows the drawing.** `transform-origin` is in user units; leaving it at the
  old shapes' corner would swing the hammer about a point in empty space.
- **Same composition.** Anvil below, head above right, spark where they meet. A mark that
  changes its silhouette every phase is not a mark; this one fills its box without becoming
  a different picture.

## Implementation notes

### Measured first

The scratch measurement script (`.tmp/measure_glyph.py`, ignored by git) reported the ink at
74% of the box across but only 52% tall, because its `HEAD` box was `(12.6, 4.6, 13.8, 7.5)`
— a corner of the head rather than the whole rect. Re-measured against the shapes the renderer
actually builds, the p_0.46.21 mark was 11.9 × 11.3 units of ink: 74% wide, 71% tall, with
the head reaching `y 1.6` and the anvil stopping at `x 1.9`. The line that mattered was
unchanged: the spark's 0.8-unit stroke was 0.90px at 18px, and the handle 1.4 units was
1.58px. The redraw is judged on the corrected bounds.

### The drawing

- **Anvil.** Face `x 1.2–11.4`, `y 9.8–11.8`; horn to `(0.2, 10.8)`; waist `x 6.4–8.6`,
  `y 11.8–13.4`; base `x 3.8–11.0`, `y 13.4–15`. Its ink now reaches the left, bottom and
  most of the right edge.
- **Hammer.** Handle `x 10.6–12.2`, `y 2.6–9.8`; head `x 8.2–14.4`, `y 0.8–3.6`; both `rx .8`.
  The handle's base centre is the pivot.
- **Spark.** Rays about `(5.2, 8.4)` — the line the head's striking edge sweeps at `-72deg` —
  at `stroke-width: 1.4` with round caps.
- The view box stays `0 0 16 16`; only the shapes inside it moved. The mark is 18 × 18px, so
  the ink is 15.98 × 15.97px (89% of each dimension), the spark is 1.57px and the handle
  1.80px wide.

The pivot in `chat.css` moves to `11.4px 9.8px`, the face's top-right corner. Swinging the
head to `-72deg` from there puts its four corners at `(1.9,10.1)`, `(3.8,4.2)`, `(4.5,10.9)`
and `(6.4,5.0)` — all inside the view box, so no frame clips.

## What was not walked

No real window here. The proportions were checked by geometry — ink bounds, stroke widths
and the rotated head's corners against the view box — not by eye; the mark's weight beside
0.85em text and the spark's flash at 18px were reasoned from the numbers.

- Changing the 18px box, the animation's timing, the verbs or the colours.
- A protocol change, a host change, a version bump, a branch, or a push.
