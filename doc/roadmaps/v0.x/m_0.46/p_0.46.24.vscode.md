# p_0.46.24 — A footprint that scrolls, and a total that cannot be covered

**Status:** done (2026-10-21)
**Milestone:** [m_0.46_editor_polish.vscode.md](../m_0.46_editor_polish.vscode.md)
**Linked design:** [46-editor-polish.vscode.md](../../../design/v0.x/46-editor-polish.vscode.md) §27,
[35-vscode-extension.vscode.md](../../../design/v0.x/35-vscode-extension.vscode.md) (the chat view)

## Goals

The root line and the run's line float in the dock, above the composer, over the transcript.
The dock reserves their height from a measurement taken by a `ResizeObserver`, so the reserve
lags the layout by a frame — during that frame the conversation is painted over the lines, or
the lines over the conversation, and a turn's growth makes it visible. p_0.46.23 reordered
them but could not remove the overlap, because the overlap is what floating means.

This phase moves each fact to where its lifetime puts it.

1. **The run's line and the root line become one entry inside `#transcript`**, under a
   `#footprint` element, so they scroll with the conversation and cannot be painted over by
   it. They sit 4px apart — the panel's existing label-to-value rhythm — and the entry takes
   the transcript's own 12px block rhythm.
2. **The block keeps its first line whether or not a run is in flight** (`min-height: 1.5em`
   on `#status`), so starting or ending a turn does not change the block's height.
3. **The conversation's context share joins the root line**, after the folder:
   `Root anvil · 848k of 1M (85%) · 152k left`.
4. **The session total moves inside `.composer`**, the one opaque surface in the dock, and is
   painted over the transcript rather than spaced away from it. It is left-aligned in the
   actions row, ellipsises before the buttons do, and keeps its whole text in `title` and
   `aria-label`.

The rule the layout follows: a fact that must never be overlapped lives inside an opaque
surface; a fact allowed to move with the conversation stays in the flow.

## Scope

`editors/vscode/src/chatPanel.ts` (the footprint in the transcript, the lines cut from the
dock, the meter into the composer), `media/chat.css` (the footprint's rhythm, the two lines'
padding removed, the composer meter), `media/chat.js` (`renderRoot` and `renderMeter` split
by scope, the footprint's visibility, scroll pinning), `test/unit/working.test.ts` and
`test/unit/usageMeter.test.ts`. No protocol field, no host change, no setting.

## Tasks

1. **Cut, do not copy.** `#status` and `#root` leave `#composerDock`; each id appears once in
   the document.
2. **Add the footprint.** `#footprint`, hidden, is the last child of `#transcript`, holding
   `#status` then `#root`; `order: 1` keeps it last however turns are appended.
3. **Give it the panel's rhythm.** `gap: 4px`, `margin-block: 4px`, `0.85em`, muted,
   `line-height: var(--ui-line-tight)`, one ellipsised line each, no horizontal padding —
   the transcript's own 12px inset applies.
4. **Reserve the first line.** `.footprint .status { min-height: 1.5em }`; the status element
   is no longer hidden by content, only the whole footprint is.
5. **Split the meter by scope.** `renderMeter` writes the conversation's remaining/left/
   compact-at facts into a `.root-facts` span on the root line, and the session's in/out and
   cache into `#meter` inside the composer. `renderRoot` rebuilds the span and asks
   `renderMeter` to fill it.
6. **Pin the scroll.** `renderRoot`, `setStatus`, `startWorking` and `stopWorking` record
   whether the reader was at the bottom before they change the block, and restore it after.
7. **Keep the block attached.** `clearTranscript` empties the transcript; it re-appends the
   footprint after the empty placeholder.
8. **Test.** The block is the transcript's last child and holds status then root; the meter
   is inside the composer's actions before the spacer; the rhythm and the reserve; one
   occurrence of each id; the two scopes' arithmetic.
9. **Verify.** Compile, lint and tests; the Go triple from Git Bash; VSIX packaging and the
   packaged smoke test.

## Decisions (before any change)

- **In-flow beats overlaying.** A measured reserve can only ever be one frame behind; putting
  the lines in the scroll content removes the race instead of widening the margin.
- **One entry, not two.** Two transcript entries would be 12px apart and read as unrelated
  facts; 4px is the panel's label-to-value gap (`.msg`'s `.who` → `.body`) and these two
  lines are a label and its value.
- **The same type size as metadata.** `0.85em` keeps them below the answer they follow, which
  is why the dock existed in the first place; promoting them to body size would undo §23.
- **The first line is reserved, not conditional.** A block that shrinks when a turn ends
  moves everything below it; an empty 16.57px line costs less than a jump.
- **The session total goes inside the opaque card.** The composer already has a background
  and a shadow; anything behind it is painted over, so the total cannot be overlapped even
  during a resize frame.
- **Left, not right.** The row's right end holds the `@`, Image and Send buttons; a
  right-aligned total would shift them as it ellipsised.
- **No new live region.** `#transcript` is `aria-live="off"`, so the per-second clock inside
  it is not announced; the end of a turn is still said once through `#srStatus`.

## Implementation notes

### The template

`#status` and `#root` left `#composerDock`; `#footprint`, hidden, is the last child of
`#transcript`, holding `#status` then `#root`. `#status` carries no `hidden` attribute of its
own — the reserve is the point, and an author `hidden` would collapse the box the stylesheet
reserves. Visibility is decided once, by `syncFootprint`, which writes `footprint.hidden`
from the three children's emptiness and replaces the three `statusLine.hidden` writes that
used to live in `setStatus`, `startWorking` and `stopWorking`.

`#meter` moved into `.composer .actions` before the spacer, so the session total is painted
on the composer rather than floated over the transcript. `#composerDock` now holds only the
two conditional banners and the form.

### The stylesheet

`.footprint` is a 4px-gap column with `margin-block: 4px` and `order: 1`, so the block sorts
last however a turn is appended. `.status` and `.root` drop the dock's `+1px` horizontal
padding and take the transcript's own inset; `.status` adds `min-height: 1.5em`. `.meter`
became `.composer .meter`: `flex: 1 1 auto`, `min-width: 0`, `0.8em`, left-aligned, ellipsised.

### The renderer

`renderMeter` now writes two scopes from one body. `meterMath.remaining` — used, percentage,
left, compact-at — goes to a `.root-facts` span that `renderRoot` creates between the folder
and the note; `meterMath.label` (only when no window is declared) and `meterMath.cache` go to
`#meter` inside the composer. The composer's half carries its untruncated text in `title`
and `aria-label`. `startWorking`, `stopWorking`, `setStatus`, `renderRoot` and `renderMeter`
all capture `atBottom()` before writing and call `scrollIfPinned` after, because every one of
them now changes the scroll content. `clearTranscript` re-appends the footprint after
`transcript.textContent = ''` detaches it.

## What was not walked

No real window here. The two lines were placed by the panel's existing rhythm rather than by
eye; the 18px working mark inside a 1.5em line box makes that line about 1.4px taller than
the reserved empty line, so the block still moves by that much when a run starts — the
reserve covers the empty-to-summary case, not the mark's own height, and only a real window
can say whether it shows. The composer meter's ellipsis under a long total and a narrow
panel was reasoned from `min-width: 0`, not seen; and where the block sits once a
conversation is long enough to scroll was reasoned from the transcript's padding, not
scrolled.

## Non-goals

- Hiding the footprint in focus view; it is the run's state, not the agent's side work.
- A new live region, a new message type, a new setting.
- Changing the mark, the verbs, the colours or the clock.
- A protocol change, a host change, a version bump, a branch, or a push.
