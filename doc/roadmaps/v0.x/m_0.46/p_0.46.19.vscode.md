# p_0.46.19 — A diff scrolls by the side, not by the line

**Status:** done (2026-10-20)
**Milestone:** [m_0.46_editor_polish.vscode.md](../m_0.46_editor_polish.vscode.md)
**Linked design:** [46-editor-polish.vscode.md](../../../design/v0.x/46-editor-polish.vscode.md) §22,
[35-vscode-extension.vscode.md](../../../design/v0.x/35-vscode-extension.vscode.md) (the chat view)

## Goals

p_0.46.10 put an edit's two sides beside each other. Each cell of each row scrolled its own
long line, so a diff wide enough to overflow drew a horizontal scrollbar under every line of
both sides — a bank of bars inside the comparison, louder than the code they belonged to.

1. **The overflow belongs to the side.** The old side and the new side are each one scrolling
   pane. A long line scrolls its pane; the other side keeps its place. One bar per side, at
   the foot of the pane, instead of one per line.
2. **The bars stay out of the way.** They are invisible until the pointer is over the
   comparison, and their track is transparent, so the diff reads as rows until a reader
   reaches for the overflow.
3. **The sides move together.** Dragging either bar, a wheel over either pane, or a vertical
   scroll moves both panes to the same offset, so the two halves of a change never drift
   apart.
4. **The narrow shape survives.** Below the same threshold as before, the diff is one
   column; the panes cannot be laid out as one column, so the comparison is repainted in the
   unified shape when the threshold is crossed.

## Scope

`editors/vscode/media/chat.js` (`renderEditDiff`, the pane scroll sync, the repaint on the
narrow threshold) and `media/chat.css` (the panes, the rows, the scrollbars); the layout,
tool-card and a new diff-pane test. The milestone page and the design chapter record the
change. No protocol field, no host change, no setting, no new message type.

## Tasks

1. **One pane per side.** `pre.diff` holds `.pane.old` and `.pane.new`; each pane holds one
   row per pair for its side, and an empty row where that side has nothing (a deletion has
   no new side, an addition no old side).
2. **Scroll as one.** `syncDiffScroll` pairs the panes' `scroll` events and copies
   `scrollTop` and `scrollLeft` to the other pane.
3. **Hide the bars until they are wanted.** 10px scrollbars with a transparent track and
   thumb; the thumb takes the editor's scrollbar colour on hover over the comparison.
4. **One vertical bar.** The old side's vertical scrollbar is 0px wide; the new side's stays
   at the right edge where a scrollbar has always been.
5. **Repaint the narrow shape.** `renderEditDiff` paints panes or a unified column depending
   on the body's `diff-narrow` class; `syncDiffWidth` repaints the diffs on screen when the
   class changes.
6. **Test.** Source assertions for the panes, the sync, the repaint and the classes; CSS
   assertions for the pane scrollers, the hidden thumb and the hover.
7. **Verify.** Compile, lint and tests; the Go triple from Git Bash; VSIX packaging and the
   packaged smoke test.

## Decisions (before any change)

- **The pane is the scroller, not the cell.** The cell scroller was the bug: it made every
  line an independent window into its own text. A pane gives the side one scrollbar and one
  scroll position, which is also what makes the two sides synchronizable at all.
- **Two panes rather than one shared horizontal scroller.** A single scroller would have to
  wrap the vertical one. With the horizontal scroller outside, the vertical bar sits at the
  right edge of the widest line, off screen until the reader scrolls to it; with it inside,
  the horizontal bar sits under the last row instead of at the foot of the box. One scroller
  per pane keeps the vertical bar on the visible edge and the horizontal bars at the foot.
- **Equal offsets, not proportional ones.** Copying `scrollLeft` (clamped by the other
  pane's own width) keeps the same character column on both sides, which is what a reader
  comparing two lines wants; scaling by the ratio of the scroll ranges would drift the
  columns apart as soon as the sides differ in width.
- **The bar appears on hover, not in the theme's scrollbar styling.** The webview's default
  scrollbar is always painted; a diff is a glance, not a document, and two permanent bars at
  the foot of every edit is the noise this phase removes. The thumb keeps the theme's colours
  when it does appear.
- **The narrow shape is a repaint.** A grid of rows can hide one cell and read as one column;
  a pane cannot, because the pane is a whole side and the unified column interleaves the two.
  Repainting is cheap (24 rows at most) and happens only when the threshold flips.

## Implementation notes

### The panes

`pre.diff` becomes a flex row of `.pane.old` and `.pane.new`. A pair contributes a row to
each pane: a `change` is a `del` row on the left and an `add` row on the right, a one-sided
pair leaves an empty row on the side that has nothing, and the left pane carries the hairline
divider. Rows are `width: max-content; min-width: 100%` so a long line widens its pane's
scroll range while a short one still paints its background across the pane.

Each pane is `overflow: scroll` with 10px custom scrollbars: transparent track and thumb
until `.diff:hover`, then the editor's `--vscode-scrollbarSlider-*` colours. The old pane's
vertical scrollbar is set to 0px wide, so the divider is the only thing between the sides and
the new pane's bar stays where a scrollbar has always been. `overflow: scroll` rather than
`auto` keeps the feet of the two panes the same height even when only one side overflows, so
the rows stay level.

### The movement

`syncDiffScroll(pre)` copies `scrollTop` and `scrollLeft` from whichever pane moved to the
other, comparing before writing so a synchronized assignment cannot echo back. The panes have
the same number of rows of the same height, so equal vertical offsets are the same line; equal
horizontal offsets are the same character column.

### The narrow repaint

`renderEditDiff` keeps its pairs and paints them from `paint()`, which reads the body's
`diff-narrow` class: panes when wide, the unified column when narrow (a context row once, the
two sides of a change stacked). The painter is kept in a `WeakMap` keyed by the `pre`, and
`syncDiffWidth` calls `repaintDiffs()` when the class flips, so an open conversation follows
the window. The scroll listeners are attached to the panes at paint time, because the panes
are new nodes after a repaint.

## What was not walked

No real window here. The hover reveal, the two bars' alignment with the pane feet, the
transparent track in the light theme, and the repaint's effect on scroll position in an open
conversation were reasoned from the declarations, not seen. The scrollbar colours in a theme
that publishes no `--vscode-scrollbarSlider-*` variables fall back to the translucent grey
written in the stylesheet.

## Non-goals

- Wrapping long lines, a wrap toggle, or a horizontal scrollbar shared by other transcript
  surfaces.
- Changing the 24-row cap, the pairing, or the line numbers.
- A protocol change, a host change, a version bump, a branch, or a push.
