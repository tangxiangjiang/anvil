# p_0.64.2 — Bounded repair, conflict proposal, and re-plan

**Status:** planned

## Goals

Resolve selected findings without mutating approved evidence or entering an autonomous repair loop.

## Scope

- Add revision-bound accept/dismiss/create-repair decisions for selected finding ids.
- Enforce attempt, elapsed-time, token, and changed-scope budgets.
- Run repairs in new isolated attempts and require fresh verification and independent review.
- Produce conflicts in disposable proposal Worktrees.
- Produce structured dependency/acceptance/policy impact diffs for explicit re-plan approval.

## Tasks

1. Implement finding decisions and repair/re-plan budget enforcement.
2. Route repairs through fresh isolated execution, verification, and independent review.
3. Add disposable conflict proposals, impact diffs, stale decisions, and recursion-stop tests.

## Acceptance

- No repair/re-plan begins without an allowed durable decision.
- Original Worker/evidence remains immutable and running work is not silently cancelled.
- Bounds stop recursion deterministically and retain every attempt.
- Conflict/re-plan proposals cannot apply themselves.

## Non-goals

- Integration, push, PR, automatic plan replacement, or unbounded fixing.

## Depends-on

- p_0.64.1 and milestones 0.61–0.63.
