# p_0.64.1 — Review artifact, evidence, and policy

**Status:** planned
**Milestone:** [m_0.64_autonomous_review_replan.vscode.md](../m_0.64_autonomous_review_replan.vscode.md)
**Linked design:** [64-autonomous-review-and-replan.vscode.md](../../../design/v0.x/64-autonomous-review-and-replan.vscode.md)

## Goals

Define immutable findings, verdicts, evidence provenance, and repository review policy.

## Scope

Review/finding schemas, patch/plan/acceptance/verification bindings, severity/category, confidence,
policy gates, revisions, storage, stale detection, and path/content redaction.

## Tasks

1. Add append-only review artifact and finding records.
2. Bind artifacts to exact Worker, commits, patch, plan, acceptance, and verification evidence.
3. Define pass/changes-requested/blocked/inconclusive policy semantics.
4. Make truncation/missing evidence explicit and policy-relevant.
5. Add JSONL/SQLite and stale/adversarial evidence tests.

## Acceptance

- Any bound change invalidates the review revision.
- A finding cites labelled evidence or is explicitly inconclusive.
- Policy cannot convert missing required evidence into pass.
- Model/source content cannot inject identities, severity gates, or authorization.

## Non-goals

- Running a reviewer or creating repair work.
