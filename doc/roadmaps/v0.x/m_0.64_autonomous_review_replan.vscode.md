# Milestone 0.64 — Autonomous review and controlled re-planning

**Status:** planned

Add role-separated, evidence-bound review and bounded repair/re-plan proposals without giving a
reviewer authority to write, integrate, publish, or rewrite plans silently. Design:
[64](../../design/v0.x/64-autonomous-review-and-replan.vscode.md).

## Goals

1. Persist immutable review artifacts and policy/verdict semantics.
2. Run read-only reviewer Agents with evidence, truncation, and stale-state gates.
3. Turn selected findings into bounded repair attempts, conflict proposals, or approved re-plans.

## Overall acceptance

- Reviewer tools cannot write, integrate, publish, or access secrets.
- Every artifact is bound to exact code, plan, verification, model/profile, and evidence digests.
- Stale/truncated/incomplete evidence cannot produce an unqualified pass.
- Repair/re-plan loops are bounded and require durable decisions.
- Integration/publication confirmations remain unchanged.

## Non-goals

- Replacing human/security review, automatic merge/conflict resolution, or recursive reviewer
  chains.
